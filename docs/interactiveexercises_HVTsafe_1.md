# Exercise 3: HVTsafe: Safe Space for Challenging Discussions 

This is an example of an exercise to help in disaster preparedness through a safe space for challenging discussions.

Many discussions around disaster actions require sensitivity for people to feel safe describing their needs.  For example Evacuation decisions are rarely simple. `HVTsafe` creates an anonymous space for residents to discuss tough household dilemmas—such as leaving animals behind, guarding homes, or caring for Elders. Using peer-mirror scoring, participants guess how their neighbors responded to help break feelings of isolation, while an anonymous community board surfaces practical solutions for Tribal planners.

*(Note: Pilot exercise data exploring how household roles shape evacuation willingness is featured in the accompanying presentation charts.)*
