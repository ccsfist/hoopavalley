# Exercise 4: HVTcoordinate--Coordinating resources before and during disasters

For this illustration, we examine the setup of and needs for resources at three shelter facilities, with people role playing different roles.  This could also be set up with non-hypothetical roles, locations and needs, informed by experts, reports, and community exercises like the ones we have already explored.

To align logistics across the entire community, `HVTcoordinate` runs three simultaneous tracks:

- **Track 1 (Residents):** Maps household valley sectors, medical power dependencies, and endurance limits.
- **Track 2 (Providers):** Audits facility generator fuel supplies and staffing bottlenecks.
- **Track 3 (Leadership):** Evaluates central hub staging versus decentralized sector supply caches.
